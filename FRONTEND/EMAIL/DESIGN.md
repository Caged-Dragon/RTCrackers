## Brand & Style
The design system powers an enterprise-grade, high-velocity email client built specifically for internal and customer operations. The brand personality balances precision engineering with vibrant kinetic energy—channeling the celebratory flash of festive fireworks into a disciplined, high-focus productivity interface.

The visual aesthetic combines Modern Corporate SaaS with Minimalist utility: ultra-sharp micro-borders, clean spatial ratios, low-distraction surface hierarchies, and energetic saffron-orange brand sparks that guide the eye directly to key workflow triggers without visual fatigue. The experience feels fast, fluid, crisp, and robust, engineered for power users handling hundreds of communications daily.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a crisp white and slate foundation with decisive bursts of chromatic energy:

- **Primary (`#EA580C`)**: An intense saffron/ember orange serving as the focal activator for primary actions, active mailbox indicators, unread indicators, and key focus states.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep midnight slate providing high-contrast textual clarity, primary structural headers, and command-bar anchoring.
- **Tertiary (`#FFBF3F`)**: Pyrotechnic amber used sparingly for alerts, stars/favoriting, pending synchronization cues, and secondary badges.
- **Neutrals (`#64748B`, `#E2E8F0`, `#F1F5F9`, `#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`)**: A layered cool-slate spectrum dedicated to data density, crisp structural dividers, and eye-strain reduction during long reading sessions.

### Mailbox Taxonomic System
Mailbox categories rely on calibrated semantic tags engineered to meet WCAG AA standards against soft tinted backgrounds (`10%` opacity fills paired with crisp solid text labels):
- `admin`: Crimson (`#DC2626`)
- `support`: Core Blue (`#2563EB`)
- `sales`: Emerald (`#16A34A`)
- `orders`: Deep Amber (`#D97706`)
- `billing`: Royal Violet (`#7C3AED`)
- `info`: Cyan Teal (`#0891B2`)
- `contact`: Indigo (`#4F46E5`)
- `notifications`: Saffron Fire (`#EA580C`)
- `noreply`: Graphite Slate (`#475569`)

## Typography
The system couples **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headers and contextual modals with **Inter** for core email interaction, thread reading, tabular navigation, and micro-labels.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** brings subtle personality, geometric balance, and warm contemporary styling to structural sections and inbox headings.
- **Inter** handles high-density body copy, subject snippets, timestamps, metadata, and keyboard shortcuts, maximizing legibility in compact vertical arrangements.
- Tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be used for timestamps, attachment counters, and unread counts to prevent layout jittering across state changes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 3-pane architecture calibrated for desktop productivity:
1. **Collapsible Folder / Account Nav Pane:** Fixed 240px width (collapsible to 64px icon rail).
2. **Compact Message Feed Pane:** Fixed 360px–420px width for scanning high-density subject lines, sender chips, and time stamps.
3. **Reading / Action Canvas:** Fluid remaining viewport width, maintaining a reading max-width of 860px to preserve comfortable scan lengths.

On tablet viewports, the interface switches to a dual-pane master-detail view with an off-canvas navigation drawer. Mobile devices collapse into a single-pane swipe-stack view with sticky primary actions. Vertical rhythms are tuned tightly: email list items maintain a dense 48px to 64px height profile to maximize above-the-fold intake.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed primarily through **tonal layers** and **hairline micro-borders** (`#E2E8F0` and `#F1F5F9`) rather than heavy dropshadows, ensuring an exceptionally crisp visual cadence.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background containers, raw reading pane surface (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Level 1 (Layered Panes & Nav Rail):** `#F8FAFC` side panels separated by a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` right divider.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Selected Items):** Selected rows use a crisp left accent border (`3px solid #EA580C`) paired with a `#FFF7ED` warm-tinted base layer.
- **Level 3 (Floating Popovers, Search Menus & Command Palette):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) reinforced by ambient, diffuse drop shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Modal Windows & Compose Dock):** Elevated overlay dock anchored bottom-right with `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.

## Shapes
A disciplined **soft** geometry (`roundedness: 1` — default `4px / 0.25rem`) sets the baseline for the application. This restrained corner radius delivers a modern, compact, tool-like precision suited for information-dense SaaS workflows.

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Table Rows:** 4px radius for crisp edge alignment.
- **Pills & Mailbox Tags:** 12px pill radius (`rounded-full`) to immediately differentiate taxonomy labels from interactive action targets.
- **Floating Modals & Compose Floating Panes:** 8px (`0.5rem`) for container panels and drop-downs.
- **Avatars:** Fully circular (`rounded-full`) to contrast against rectangular message lines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Ember Orange (`#EA580C`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, subtle 1px border overlay (`rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`). On hover: `#C2410C`. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#EA580C`.
- **Secondary:** White (`#FFFFFF`) background, `#0F172A` text, 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). On hover: `#F8FAFC` background with border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost / Icon Actions:** Transparent background, `#64748B` icon fill. On hover: `#F1F5F9` background, `#0F172A` icon fill.

### Mailbox Tags & Filter Chips
- Rendered with an ultra-light tint background (`10%` alpha of tag color), matching solid color text, and a micro-dot indicator (`6px x 6px` solid circle).
- Height: 20px; font size: 11px, weight: 600.
- Clickable chips include a close cross or active status outline.

### Email List Items
- Unread threads: Bold subject typography (`title-sm`), high-contrast midnight text (`#0F172A`), with a solid `#EA580C` 6px unread dot on the left.
- Read threads: Normal weight text (`body-sm`), slate metadata (`#64748B`), pure white background.
- Selected state: Subtle warm saffron tint (`#FFF7ED`) with a 3px active indicator along the left edge.
- Compact layout displays avatar, sender, subject preview, timestamp, and colored category pills in an uncluttered single or dual row.

### Checkboxes & Bulk Selectors
- Custom 16px square with 3px border radius.
- Unchecked: 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, white background.
- Checked / Indeterminate: Solid `#EA580C` fill with white checkmark glyph.

### Search & Command Bar
- Global quick-search input with a persistent shortcut trigger chip (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`).
- Inactive: `#F1F5F9` fill, no border, `#64748B` placeholder.
- Active / Focused: `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #EA580C`, paired with a deep shadow popover displaying search history, category filters, and quick contacts.

### Compose Dock
- Docked at the bottom-right of the viewport with minimize, expand, and close tools in the header (`#0F172A`).
- Rich text toolbar nestled cleanly between subject line and message canvas with micro icon buttons and clear divider rules.
