Full Policy Details

Terms, Cancellation & Refunds

  • Minimum order value: ₹2,000/- (after discount).
  • Flat shipping fee of ₹250 is applicable for purchases between ₹2,000 and ₹9,999.
  • Free delivery for order value equal to or more than ₹10,000/-.
  • A flat shipping fee of ₹250 will be added for ALL orders to Chennai and Kumbakonam, regardless of order value.
  • For the rest of Tamil Nadu, the delivery fee is paid by the customer to the transporter directly while collecting the parcel from the nearest transport office.
  • All eligible discounts and promotions are applied as stated on the website and automatically calculated towards the bill amount.
  • All orders are processed only after payment realization.

This website does not process online payments. Order requests are submitted for confirmation. The order will be processed according to RT Crackers' payment and order-confirmation procedure.

  • Consignment will be shipped ONLY to the address confirmed while placing the order.
  • If you want items shipped to a different location, you must inform customer support within 6 hours of placing the order — otherwise items will be shipped to the original delivery address.
  • A maximum of 2 delivery attempts will be made.
  • If you are unavailable to receive the parcel after 2 attempts, the order will be cancelled and no refund will be given in that scenario.
  • Management reserves the right to cancel an order in case of unforeseen events. In this case, payment will be refunded within 15 working days.
  • Order amendments are not allowed once payment has been made.
  • Customers may cancel an order only within 24 hours of the order date/time. A cancellation fee of 25% of the order amount will be charged, and the balance will be refunded within 15 working days.
  • Customers must verify that the invoice matches the order details and that the parcel is received in good condition at the time of delivery.
  • After delivery confirmation, no returns or claims will be entertained under any circumstances.
  • Any items found to be missing at the time of delivery will either be replaced within 3–4 days, or a proportional amount will be refunded within 15 days.

RTCrackers reserves the right to dispatch substitute products when originally ordered items are not available due to unavoidable circumstances.
