Diwali & Crackers

A few interesting facts about the festival, shared as general information rather than marketing claims.

A Public Holiday in 10 Countries

Beyond India, Diwali/Deepavali is a declared public holiday in countries including Pakistan (Sindh Province), Nepal, Singapore, Malaysia, Myanmar, Guyana, Mauritius, Fiji, Sri Lanka, Suriname and Trinidad and Tobago.

Celebrated Across Four Religions

Diwali holds significance for Hindus, Sikhs, Jains and Newar Buddhists, each with their own traditions tied to the festival.

A Five-Day Festival

Traditionally, Diwali spans five days: Dhan Teras, Kali Chaudas (Choti Diwali), the main Diwali/Deepavali day, Bestu Baras (New Year in Gujarat), and Bhai Dooj.

A Long History of Fireworks

The earliest documented fireworks date to 7th-century China. Fireworks for celebration were first recorded in India in the 16th century, and today major fireworks displays also mark Guy Fawkes Day in the UK and Independence Day in the USA.
