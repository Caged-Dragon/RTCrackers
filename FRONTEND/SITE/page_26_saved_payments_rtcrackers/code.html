Payment Inquiries & Policies

What payment methods are supported on RTCrackers? expand_more

We accept all major Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Diners Club, American Express), all UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred), Net Banking across 55+ Indian banks, as well as split wallet options and Cash on Delivery for select pin-codes.

How does RBI card tokenization protect my financial details? expand_more

Under the Reserve Bank of India’s Card-on-File Tokenization (CoFT) mandate, merchants do not store your actual 16-digit card digits or expiry credentials. Instead, a unique surrogate identifier ("token") is generated by the card network specifically for RTCrackers transactions.

Is Cash on Delivery (COD) supported alongside saved methods? expand_more

Yes, COD is available for orders under ₹15,000 across serviceable domestic pincodes. However, festival-grade custom bat knocking and personalized laser engraving services require pre-authorization via saved digital instruments.

What should I do if money was debited but the order confirmation is pending? expand_more

Interbank handshakes may occasionally encounter network timeouts. If your account was debited without immediate confirmation, our system reconciles logs within 15 minutes. Unsettled amounts are automatically reversed back to the source account within 2-4 business days.
