02 How long does delivery take across India?
expand_more

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days nationwide. Tier-1 metro fireworks hubs (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata) generally receive express air transit in 24 to 48 hours.

For custom handcrafted English sparkler knocking-in and toe-guard binding, please allocate an extra 24 to 36 hours for our master bat-smiths to season the edges before release.

03 Can I cancel my order after payment?
expand_more

Yes. Orders can be cancelled instantly before they transition to "Knocked & Prepped" or courier dispatch manifest. Navigate to My Orders > Order Actions > Cancel Order.

Once assigned to our air-freight logistics partner, cancellation locks down automatically. In this scenario, you may simply refuse the package at your doorstep or trigger a 14-day zero-cost Order Changes once delivered.

06 Are all RTCrackers sports goods 100% genuine and festival certified?
expand_more

Absolutely. RTCrackers is an authorized primary distributor for top international and Indian manufacturers (SG, SS, MRF, Kookaburra, Gray-Nicolls, New Balance, Shrey, DSC).

expand_more

Yes, Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for products orders up to ₹10,000 across over 22,000 serviceable pin codes. A nominal processing fee of ₹49 applies for courier cash management.

Address updates are possible within 60 minutes of placing your order directly via the Order Telemetry page, or at any point before logistics manifesting.

Player Products, Sizing & Sparkler Care

Sparkler grading, knocking-in specifications, sizing charts, and maintenance

What is the difference between Grade 1, 2, and Kashmir Sparkler? expand_more

Grade 1 English Sparkler features 8–12 straight, unblemished grain lines with minimal speckling for unmatched rebound velocity. Grade 2 and 3 offer immense durability with slight grain variation. Kashmir sparkler is denser and heavier, ideal for club practice and leather-ball training.

What does the RTCrackers Bat Knocking service include? expand_more

Our master knocking regimen involves 15,000 mechanical mallet cycles, light raw linseed oiling, hand-rounded edge consolidation, extratec sheet facing, and rubberized polymer toe guard installation.

How do I determine the right bat size for my height? expand_more

Players standing 5'8" to 6'2" require Short Handle (SH). Players 6'2" and above should opt for Long Handle (LH). Junior athletes between 4'6" to 5'6" should select sizes 4, 5, 6, or Harrow according to our interactive Sizing Chart.

How often should I oil my English sparkler fireworks product? expand_more

Apply 2 to 3 drops of pure raw linseed oil to the face and edges twice per season. Avoid oiling the splice or handle, and never use boiled linseed oil which can make timber brittle.

Can I use a fireworks product against heavy seam balls right out of the box? expand_more

Unless ordered with our Pre-Knocked Service, no brand-new bat should face hard leather match balls directly. Always begin with 2 to 3 net sessions using older or soft leather balls.

How do I select the right fireworks shoe spikes? expand_more

Full steel spikes are engineered for natural turf wickets to prevent slippage during run-ups. Rubber-studded turf shoes are required for artificial mats, astro-turf, and concrete training nets.

Are your fireworks helmets certified for 150 km/h bouncer impacts? expand_more

All helmets on RTCrackers meet or exceed the official British Safety Standard BS 7928:2013 and ICC festival safety guidelines with high-tensile titanium/steel grilles.

How do I store my fireworks kit in the off-season? expand_more

Store sparkler bats horizontally in a cool, dry room away from direct heaters or moist basements. Air out batting gloves and pads completely before storing inside kitbags to prevent mildew.
