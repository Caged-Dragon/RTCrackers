Frequently Asked Questions

Comprehensive answers addressing shipment windows, bat conditioning, exchange limits, and payment processing.

For metro centers (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad), sports products and fireworks products ship via express courier within 48 to 72 hours. Regional districts and Tier-2/3 towns typically arrive within 4 to 6 business days.

Specialized hazardous material fireworks or pressurized festival equipment are transported via authorized surface linehaul trucks with licensed safe escorts, strictly adhering to PESO legal directives.

We provide a seamless 7-day Order Supportment window on all unused products (batting pads, wicketkeeping gloves, helmets, festive shoes, jerseys) in original pristine condition with manufacturer tags intact.

Note: Fireworks bats that have been knocked, oiled, personalized with name engraving, or hit against leather balls during match practice cannot be Order Changesed unless an internal sparkler cleft manufacturing fault is verified by our inspection team.

We accept all major payment avenues: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Domestic and International Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, American Express), Net Banking across 50+ Indian banks, and select verified EMI plans.

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available on fireworks soft goods up to ₹5,000. Higher-value English Sparkler items and firework consignments require prepaid verification due to courier transit regulations.

Our comprehensive factory preparation involves raw linseed oil application, a 24-hour drying period, 15,000 to 20,000 mechanical mallet strikes calibrated around edge profiles and sweet spots, followed by heavy-duty toe guard fixing and scuff sheet adhesion.

This comprehensive procedure adds approximately 2 to 3 working days before shipment dispatch to guarantee match-ready resilience against high-velocity seam bowling.

You can instantly cancel your order through your Player Account Dashboard or by calling our Toll-Free Helpline (+91 1800 266 7890) as long as the package status remains in "Processing" or "Pending Assembly".

Once an order has entered "Hand-Knocking Applied" or "Airway Bill Generated" with the courier, cancellation is subject to a nominal reverse-logistics or workshop labor charge. payment adjustment are processed back to the original source within 3-5 bank business days.
