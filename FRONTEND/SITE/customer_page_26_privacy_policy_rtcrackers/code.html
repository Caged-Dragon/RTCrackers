01

Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Red Thunder Crackers (operated by Red Thunder Pyrotechnics Limited, registered in Sivakasi, Tamil Nadu, India). We recognize that purchasing celebratory pyrotechnics demands uncompromising trust—not merely regarding the chemical safety and acoustic intensity of our crackers, but equally regarding the privacy and integrity of your digital footprint.

This Privacy Policy details the exact mechanisms through which Red Thunder Crackers ("RTCrackers", "we", "us", or "our") collects, holds, transfers, and processes your personally identifiable information (PII) across our official web portal, checkout applications, telephone reservations, and customer support channels.

By accessing our platform, configuring an order, verifying your identity, or transacting via our digital storefront, you express full informed consent to the data governance practices described within this notice, designed in strict alignment with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and applicable statutory guidelines under the Information Technology Act 2000.

04

Statutory & PESO Hazmat Compliance

local_fire_department
Mandatory Regulatory Disclosure: Pyrotechnic items fall under UN Class 1.4G Dangerous Goods regulations. Commercial dispatch requires explicit legal declarations.

In strict accordance with the Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organization (PESO) guidelines and the Indian Explosives Rules, 2008, consignor companies must generate tamper-evident transport manifests for every fireworks parcel transiting state highways or municipal check-posts.

Consequently, your consignee name, delivery address, contact mobile number, and ordered pyrotechnic net explosive quantity (NEQ) are automatically shared with certified hazmat logistics carriers (such as BlueDart Hazmat Express and specialized dedicated road freight lines) and, upon lawful subpoena, state fire safety authorities.

07

Hazmat Logistics & Delivery

Unlike conventional retail commerce, pyrotechnic shipments require strict custody transfer. When you confirm an order with Red Thunder Crackers:

  • Your physical location coordinates and delivery contact details are supplied to licensed carrier drivers with specialized hazardous materials commercial licenses.
  • Delivery is contingent on adult identity verification. Drivers are instructed to record an electronic proof of delivery (e-POD) confirming receipt by an adult occupant (18+).
  • No GPS telemetry or delivery logs are shared with third-party advertising brokers under any circumstance.
