Standard air-cargo dispatch takes 2 to 4 business days nationwide. For major metros (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata), Match-Day Express deliveries ship within 24 hours. Custom bat machine-knocking adds 24 hours of preparation.

You can freely cancel any standard order prior to its "Dispatched" transition directly from your My Orders dashboard. Instant full payment adjustment are credited immediately back to your original source instrument.

Initiate a Order Changes within 14 days of delivery, provided the item is in completely unused match condition with all manufacturer laser seals, grip tags, and warranty barcodes intact. Bats knocked by customer preference are eligible for replacement if structural flaws are confirmed.

UPI and Net Banking reimbursements are credited in 2 to 4 hours once the Order Changesed equipment passes inspection at our hub. Credit and Debit card reversals take 3 to 5 banking days, subject to your card issuer's clearing cycle.

We support Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Net Banking across 55+ Indian banking institutions, and Cash on Delivery (COD) on eligible pin codes up to ₹15,000.

Do not worry. Banking gateways periodically hold interrupted settlements. If an order confirmation is not automatically triggered within 30 minutes, your bank will automatically reverse the total sum within 24 to 48 hours. You can submit your Bank UTR code via our ticket desk for expedited manual reconciliation.
